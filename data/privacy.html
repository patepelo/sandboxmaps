Privacy policy

Sandbox Maps respects your privacy. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not identify you and does not collect personal data.

Your bookmarks, tracks, search history and settings stay on your device.

The app connects to the internet for these things only:

  • Downloading and updating maps. Maps come from the public CoMaps map servers. Like any web server, they can see your IP address and which maps you download.
  • Editing OpenStreetMap. If you sign in with an OpenStreetMap account and edit a place, your edits are sent to OpenStreetMap and are public under your account, like any OpenStreetMap contribution.

Sandbox Maps has no support email and does not receive log files or any other data from the app.

If you have any questions, please open an issue at github.com/patepelo/sandboxmaps.

Based on the CoMaps privacy policy.
Policy effective: 2026-11-19
